## Scope

Whole tool, single page (`index.html`). Mode: Operate. Audience: IFFar EaD teachers and tutors. Task: load Moodle log (and optional completion export), see who may need contact, read the class week by week, compare offerings, relate access to completion, export or print.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A follow-up tool that opens on who may need mediation, not on a dashboard of totals. It refuses the generic analytics grid of KPI tiles and charts; the class's students are the content, grouped by access pattern, each group with its suggested mediation.

OWN-WORLD: The IFFar AVA (Moodle) shell: solid green bar (#2e8b57), cream ground (#f4f1dc), white cards with warm hairline borders (#d9d4b8), deep green numbers (#1f5f3b), AVA-style tabs underlined in green. System UI sans, one family. Pattern tags as soft pills: red for withdrawal, amber for decline/concentrated/irregular, green for regular. Weekly matrix in five green tints from cream to AVA green.

STORY: The teacher understands in one look which students stopped or are fading, reads why (days since last access, weeks with access), copies the list to write to them, and can check the whole class in the weekly diary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green bar with tool name, active class and "Carregar arquivos". Tabs row: Quem precisa de contato (active), Diário semanal, Comparar turmas, Conclusão de atividades; class selector at right. Four class numbers in a row. Below, four equal columns by pattern on the cream ground, each with count, suggested strategy, student cards and "Copiar lista".

FORM: Tabs opening on triage, position 1 of 3 in the second round (user steered a combination of the diary and triage structures). seed: 86a6b0fcf021039d. Signature move: the weekly diary as a "diário de classe" matrix, student × week, tint = days with access.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
